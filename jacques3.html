JACQUES KNAVE


Jacques' signature spell is called "Folding Hand". As soon as it's casted, sparkles and
fireworks explode right where Jacques stood, as he himself seems to completely
disappear from the scene. The spell grants him temporary invisibility, and works as an
almost harmless way for him to get out of tricky situations easily, leaving the opponent off guard.
It's unclear wether the sparks from his spell only cause minor damage or pose as an actual threat.
