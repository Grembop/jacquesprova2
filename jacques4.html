TRIVIA



• Both his first and last name are a reference to the Jack card.


◦ His first name, Jacques, could be a play on Jacquese, a term utilized to refer
to a playing hand holding both a Jack and a Queen.




• It's unclear wether he's the twisted version of the Knave of Hearts
or that of a Flamingo posing as him.


◦ During the game he's shown being able to transform into a flamingo multiple times,
although his dorm members are completely unaware. In one of his vignettes he
quietly speaks to the dorm's flamingos, saying he will soon free them in the forest
outside Heartslabyul.


◦ His blonde hair could be a reference to Alice in Wonderland's Jack of hearts, shown to be yellow.




• Jacques follows a capricious diet full of sugars that seemingly ends up making his face
puffy or his body gain minor weight at times, where he then promptly switches his diet
with a more healthy and nutritious one until he's back to where he started.


◦ He's considerably embarrassed over having to switch to a healthy diet, as shown in Book 3,
where his friends Koan and Yago poke fun at him for it.




• He's admitted that when hit by Koan and Yago's ball as a kid, resulting in him crying, he
wouldn't have told them and would've simply kept sobbing quietly in the corner, so
Koan and Yago would have had no reason to prevent him from telling the teachers.




• Koan and Yago state that however they initially intended to be Jacques' friends solely
for the money, they quickly got emotionally attached to him and started appreciating
Jacques for who he is, developing a sincere bond.




• He enjoys collecting all kinds of tea sets with a deep rooted interest in fancy, handmade decors.




• He got his swordsman skills thanks to Koan's teachings from when they were little.


◦ His use of the sword might reference the Knave of Hearts from Tim Burton's Alice in Wonderland (2010)




• He's one of the few students who wasn't negatively affected by following the Queen of Hearts
rules to the letter, going so far as saying he wouldn't mind to keep following them.




• He wasn't happy about being assigned as the Jack until Riddle became housewarden.




• His routine is unpredictable to the point where he's scheduled to go feed the ducklings
at the lake, nuzzle his face into rabbits, chase them, and play cards with
the dorm's flamingos.




• He writes a letter to his housewarden once a day, although the contents of such is never specified.


◦ During Book 1, when confronted about it, he displays an embarrassed reaction.


◦ This habit is so frequent that in Riddle's vignette he shows unease when his letter
seemed to not arrive yet, it being so unusual that he starts considering
checking up on Jacques.



• His favorite dressing style is vintage victorian-esque, but he also seems to enjoy a more dark gothic aesthetic.




• He has a habit to absentmindedly raise one of his legs at times, akin to a flamingo.




• He's prone to having speech difficulties (stuttering, muttering) when
he speaks out loud after long periods of silence.




• He expresses love and gratitude with expensive gifts or treats towards his friends.




• Jacques adores having tea parties or sleepovers with his friends Koan, Yago, and even Riddle.




• In his dream in Book 7, it shows that he's unable to imagine his actual parents being
nice and supportive, replacing their physical selves with the singular picture
he used to have in his house of them.




• Jacques has a hidden birthmark in the shape of two wings on his lower back, as shown in his summer card.


◦ Koan and Yago coincidentally also have such birthmark, however placed
respectively in their middle and upper back.




• He admitted to having been mistaken for a girl multiple times during his early teen years and childhood.




• Despite his reserved demeanor, he's described to be physically affectionate towards his cherished ones.




• He's incredibly fond of anything or anyone that's cute.




• Jacques occasionally practices ice skating, however he states he's not the best at it.
