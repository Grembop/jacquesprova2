Appearance


Jacques is a fair-skinned young man whose height is just above average. His eyes are a
burnt orange shade, and on his left cheek is a small, red marking shaped like a heart. His
hair is light blonde, relatively long compared to his peers, which he keeps in a loose
ponytail resting on his right shoulder, tied with a bright red ribbon. His bangs are side
parted on the left side of his face. He's often shown with a neutral, lost in thought
expression, bordering on a judgmental glare. However when confronted directly, he often
shows a face of discomfort or embarrassment.




Dorm Uniform


His dorm uniform is similar to the rest of the Heartslabyul students. While in uniform, he
wears eyeshadow the same color as his heart-shaped marking. The uniform's top
consists of an outer jacket, a patterned vest, and a white, scoop-neck undershirt. The
jacket is mainly white with a yellow trim and red underlining. His right sleeve is black, while
his left has a black and white checkerboard pattern, both revealing a pair of short, black
gloves on his hands. The lapels of the jacket are shaped like a
heart, and are asymmetrically colored black and red. Two yellow shoulder cords hang from his left
shoulder strap, resembling those worn by army soldiers. On his left lapel is a white rose,
halfway covered in red paint. Also on the left side of his jacket is a gold, heart-shaped
brooch with a yellow "QH" inside, and a printed symbol of Heartslabyul. There is also a
playing card displaying the Knave of Hearts, a card unique to Jacques' uniform.
Heartslabyul vests can either be red or black in color; Jacques chooses to wear a red one.
The vest is patterned with all four card suits, as well as crowns and roses. Wrapped
around his waist is a yellow and black striped sash, tied together on his left side under a
white circular pin, with red ruffles and a yellow "H" in the center. His pants are white to
match the jacket, with a yellow stripe running down the outer seams. Under the pants are
black, simple elegant shoes, making him the only dorm member to wear a different pair of
shoes not belonging to the original dorm uniform.
